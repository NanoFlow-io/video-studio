# Owner Featured: Field Notes v1, how to ask Nano

Short form (Nano writes the lines and shows them with the quote):

    Use the Owner Featured template for <business> and make it about <topic>.

Long form (your own lines are used as given):

    Make an Owner Featured reel for <business>: <title>.
    1. Customer reaction: <who, where, the visible reaction>. Owner voiceover: "<one line>"
    2. Owner intro: "<one or two short lines>"
    3. Explainer: <plain-English Field Notes brief: problem, cause, fix, realistic outcome>

Default: part 3 is a NEW Field Notes story and animation for this topic. To reuse an earlier middle instead, say
"Explainer: reuse project <name>". Part 4 is the business's saved close; the music is the shared NanoFlow bed unless
the business has its own. A business needs a ready profile first (presenter, voice, logo, number, saved close).
