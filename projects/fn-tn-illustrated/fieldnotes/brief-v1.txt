TRUE NORTH: COOL BEFORE PEAK
Business: True North, a local HVAC company.
Audience: Orange County residents on time-of-use electricity plans.
Duration: approximately 15 seconds, vertical 1080x1920.

Beat 1: House and outdoor AC unit. Show AC activity overlapping an ochre peak-price window on a timeline.
Beat 2: Show pre-cooling before the peak window. Blue cooling fills the house; move some cooling activity earlier along the timeline.
Beat 3: Raise the thermostat setting slightly during peak hours and illustrate reduced AC activity then. Label: "Check your rate plan."

Use the same house, AC unit and timeline consistently. Do not reuse the unrelated underground cutaway or protection shield.
No invented rates, universal peak times, numerical savings or guarantees of lower total electricity use.

NARRATION TIMING SCRIPT
"On a time-of-use plan? Cool your home before peak rates, then raise the thermostat a little. That can shift some cooling away from expensive hours. Check your plan's peak times."
