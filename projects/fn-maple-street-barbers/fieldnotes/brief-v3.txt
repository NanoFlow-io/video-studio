FICTIONAL TEST: Maple Street Barbers, a neighbourhood barber shop. Audience: regular customers who book online.
Problem: some booked customers don't turn up, so a barber's chair sits empty at a busy time.
Cause: the only confirmation is an email sent at booking, often days earlier, and it gets buried under other notifications.
Solution: a text the day before that the customer can reply to if they can't make it.
Outcome: when someone replies, the shop can offer that chair time to someone on the walk-in list.
End on a checklist card that says: Reply if you can't make it.
About 15 seconds.
